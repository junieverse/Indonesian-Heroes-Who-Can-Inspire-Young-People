Indonesian Heroes Who Can Inspire Young People


Raden Adjeng Kartini

Raden Adjeng Kartini or often we call Kartini, is a woman from Jepara who was born on April 21, 1879. She is one of many Indonesian heroes who fought for Indonesian—especially for women of Indonesia. She is the embodiment of the struggle against patriarchy. She fought for young women to have an education.

She is one of the reason why we can have education and equality. She inspires many people—especially young women to fight for gender equality. She inspires many girls to finally reach their dreams or build a career purely from her own choices. This is why she is one of many role models who can inspire the young people that wants to build their own lives in their own good way.

Sutomo

Sutomo or familiarly called by Bung Tomo, is an activist from Indonesia. He fought against the dutch colonial rule. He is the one of the leader who lead the Battle of Surabaya and known as the Hero's Day. The event led by Bung Tomo became a national symbol of Indonesian resistance against colonialism and imperialism.

He can inspires young people to fight for their rights. He inspires many young people to fight for their own freedom. His burning spirit and tireless struggle can serve as an inspiration for Indonesia's youth to carry on his legacy and build a bright future for the nation. One of the role model that needs to be more appreciated.

Dewi Sartika

Dewi Sartika is an advocate for and pioneer of education for women in Indonesia. She built the first school for women in the Dutch East Indies. She built a school that was named "Sakola Kaoetamaan Isteri" and later was change to "Sakola Raden Dewi" to to commemorate her legacy. She is one of the heroes who fought for women's literacy and freedom in Indonesia.

She is one fo the role models for young people—especially young women. Her dedication to built the first school for women is impossible to imagine how difficult it is. This inspires young people to pursue your dreams no matter how hard or when people tell you that it's impossible. As youngsters, we need to continue her legacy to fought for women's literacy and freedom.
